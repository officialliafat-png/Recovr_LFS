The problem

Students

Not knowing whether an item was found, who to ask, or where to report it.

Staff

Re-answering the same questions and matching reports to objects by hand.

Institutions

No single view of what is lost, returned or left unclaimed.

How it works

Report it

A student reports a lost item with a photo, zone and date. Staff log found items the same way.

Match it

Computer vision compares photos and details, then ranks likely matches with a plain-language explanation.

Verify it

The claimant answers a private question. Staff approve, reject or ask for more.

Recover it

Only after approval are pickup details released, and the return is logged.

Built for institutions

Staff console

Pending claims, possible matches, unclaimed inventory and storage locations in one view.

Analytics

Return rate, average recovery time, top lost categories and high-loss campus zones.

Accountability

An audit log records reports, matches, claims and returns.

Privacy

Contact details and storage locations stay private. Each institution sees only its own data.

The Match Score is a similarity heuristic that helps staff prioritise, not a guarantee. Staff always make the final decision.
